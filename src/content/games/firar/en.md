*Firar* means "escape". You flee from the police with a family on a speedboat. Ahead lies a stretch of water full of rocks, and your goal is to survive 100 seconds without sinking.

Going slow is not enough: you have to reach the goal before the police catch you, and turbo is tempting but makes the boat harder to control. Waves give you the chance to get airborne: jump at the right moment and you fly over the rocks.

**Controls:** A / D or arrow keys to steer, hold Shift to speed up.
