Let Him Cook is an arcade game that tests your multitasking. Dishes with different sizes, cooking times and point values line up in a random queue; your job is to cook them all right and send them out before the queue overflows.

There is no timer, but bad planning and pies that just won't cook are your worst enemies. The tray plays like Tetris: rotate shapes to fit them, stoke the fire, pull them out before they burn.

**Controls:** left click to move, mouse wheel to rotate, TAB to put the next dish on the tray, Space to stoke the fire.
