You are Aiden, the only keeper of the watch tower on the Kingdom of Varenhal's Leeway strait. Every ship that wants through calls on the radio and faxes its papers. You compare the length, hull colour and registry on paper with what the binoculars, the radar and the state registry computer tell you, then press approve or reject on the document reader; a rejection needs its reasons marked.

Ships are impatient. Each one waiting calls on its own line, and an unanswered one leaves; juggling five lines, a fax and the ship in your binoculars is the job. The day moves with your decisions, not the clock.

When the weather turns, so does the work. On stormy days the things worth noticing are not only ships, and whatever you ignore has a price.

Umut and I are developing it in Unity with URP; the ships, the sea, the weather and most of the sound are generated in code.
