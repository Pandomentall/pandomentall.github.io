Ellam is a moon under constant Parlamonium rain. Nothing built on its surface lasts: asteroid showers and the **Parlavan**, semi-biological creatures that feed on the ore, wipe everything away. Aevum Extraction Group's answer sits in orbit: land, extract, leave.

You are a POD officer and every run is a shipment. Drop your miners, raise the shield, hold the line with turrets and towers, and decide how long to stay as the Parlavan swarm grows. Every Parlavan type asks a question, and no single defence answers them all.

Still in development. Devlogs will gather here as it grows.
