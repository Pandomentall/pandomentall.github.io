A link page I built for Kumpircim Blue Waffle, a kumpir and waffle shop. Customers land on one address and, with two buttons, either rate the shop on Google Maps or follow it on Instagram.

A single HTML and CSS file: a layered night sky with shooting stars, the shop's orange and blue logo, and two big buttons that are easy to tap on a phone. It runs on GitHub Pages, serverless and free.
