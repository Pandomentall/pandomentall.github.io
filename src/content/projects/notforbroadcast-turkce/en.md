Not For Broadcast is a subtitle-heavy game where you make censorship and editing calls at a live broadcast desk. This patch translates the whole game and every DLC into Turkish.

I handled text extraction, translation, font adaptation and re-injection end to end, and built a one-click installer. The patch is free and open source.
